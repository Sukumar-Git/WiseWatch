What this can and can’t do
  • Search is rule-based: synonyms, typo tolerance, object names, time offsets and clock times. It is not an AI language model, so unusual phrasing may not be understood.
  • The detector finds objects (person, car, bus, truck, bag…). Falls, running, collisions, unattended objects, entering/leaving and people staying in place are possible events inferred from how boxes move between sampled frames. They can be wrong and are shown as candidates for human review.
  • “Suspicious”, fights, theft, weapons and faces are not detected.
  • Clock-time search needs the recording start time. It is read from the file when possible, otherwise estimated, and you can correct it.
  • Browsers play MP4 (H.264) reliably. AVI and some MOV/HEVC files may not play; the page will show a conversion command.
  • Use only footage you have permission to analyse.
