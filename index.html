About WiseWatch

What it does well, and where it needs a human.

What it detects

People, vehicles, bags and other everyday objects, using YOLOv8n running in your browser.

What it infers

Possible falls, running, collisions, unattended objects, entering or leaving the view, and people staying in place. These come from how boxes move between sampled frames, so treat each as a candidate to review.

What it can’t do

Judge intent. Suspicious behaviour, fights, theft, weapons and faces aren’t detected, and named places such as “parking area” aren’t known.

How search works

Rules, not an AI language model: synonyms, typo tolerance, object names, time offsets and clock times.

Clock times and playback

Clock searches use the recording start time, read from the file when possible. MP4 (H.264) plays reliably; AVI and some MOV files need converting, and WiseWatch shows the command.

Your footage

Analysis happens on your device and nothing is uploaded. Use only footage you have permission to analyse.
